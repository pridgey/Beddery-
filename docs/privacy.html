Beddery Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

Beddery turns a charging phone into a bedside display. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Almost everything it does happens on your phone. This page lists every case where data leaves it.

What leaves your phone

  • Weather. To show the forecast, Beddery sends the latitude and longitude of the weather location you chose to Open-Meteo. No account, device ID or other information is included.
  • City search. When you search for a weather location by name, the text you type is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service to find matching places.
  • Settings backup. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Beddery's settings (your screensaver choices, schedule and weather location) are included in your Google backup so they come back on a new phone. You control this in your phone's backup settings.
  • Updates (GitHub version only). The version of Beddery downloaded from GitHub checks GitHub for newer releases. The Google Play version doesn't. Google Play handles its updates.
  • Tips (optional). In the Google Play version, tips are paid through Google Play, which handles the payment under Google's privacy policy. Beddery never sees your payment details. The GitHub version's tip link opens Ko-fi in your browser. Tips don't unlock anything.

Open-Meteo's own policy is at open-meteo.com/en/terms.

What stays on your phone

Each of these is optional and off until you turn it on. None of it is stored anywhere but your phone, and none of it is sent anywhere.

  • Location. Used once, when you tap "Use my location", to pick a weather location. Beddery only asks for approximate location and doesn't track you.
  • Calendar. Read by the Agenda screensaver to show your upcoming events.
  • Photos. The Photo Frame screensaver shows only the photos you pick with Android's photo picker. Beddery can't see the rest of your library.
  • Notifications and music. If you give Beddery notification access, screensavers can show icons or previews of new notifications, and what's playing. Silent and private notifications are respected.
  • Charging and alarms. Beddery reads battery status and your next alarm time to show them on screen and to know when to start.

Sharing and selling

Beddery doesn't collect personal data, so there's nothing to share or sell.

Children

Beddery isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes
